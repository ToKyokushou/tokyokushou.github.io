summary_en: Sustainable urban planning requires forecasts that integrate interrelated factors such as building density, building height, road networks and historical development patterns. We propose MMCN, an urban layout forecasting method that integrates multiple conditional inputs, spatial memory of neighbouring regions and temporal history into a diffusion model, and we construct a multi-temporal, multi-modal urban dataset covering Shenzhen from 2005 to 2024. Compared with existing methods, MMCN improves prediction accuracy and spatial continuity.
