summary_en: To prepare training data for generative AI across the stages of architectural design, we propose a method for extracting sketches at different levels of detail (LoD) from 3D architectural models. From 150 building models represented at three levels of detail, we render multi-view images and create progressive sketches by combining contour extraction with generative-AI-based simplification, constructing a dataset of 75,600 images that preserves correspondences across LoDs and viewpoints.
