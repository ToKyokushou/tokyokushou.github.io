We combine retrieval-augmented generation with multi-stage diffusion models to controllably generate building representations aligned with the design intent of campus buildings.

  caption: Fig. 2 This work aims to bridge abstract early-stage architectural design concepts (expressed via text prompts) with realistic architectural renderings by utilizing sketches as intermediate representations. Architectural design concepts (from text prompts) are first transformed into controlled vertical sketches through the vertical configuration control stage, incorporating the RAG method. Next, these sketches are refined and enhanced with architectural components during the semantic component resolution stage, resulting in component-controlled sketches. Finally, the component-controlled sketches and text prompts are used as inputs to generate the style-controlled rendering.
